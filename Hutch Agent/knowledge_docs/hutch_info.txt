HUTCH — MOBILE PLANS AND SERVICES KNOWLEDGE BASE
=================================================

Hutch is one of Sri Lanka's leading mobile operators, connecting over 95 percent of Sri Lankans with its island-wide Hutch 4G network. Hutch is part of CK Hutchison Holdings, a global conglomerate operating in more than 50 markets whose telecommunications division serves over 150 million subscriptions worldwide. Hutch's tagline is "There's Always an Option."

Hutch offers both prepaid and postpaid mobile connections in Sri Lanka, covering data, voice and SMS plans, annual plans, bundled combo plans, and business/enterprise solutions. Hutch customers can get a new SIM delivered to their door, pick one up from a Hutch shop or a DIY partner channel such as Keells or Food City, or order an eSIM online.

Hutch customers manage their account through the Hutch Self-Care App, available on Google Play, the Apple App Store, and the Huawei AppGallery. The Hutch App is used to activate plans, check balances, and manage add-ons. The cliQ app is a separate app used specifically for Hutch's time-based Break Free Internet plans.

CONTACT AND SUPPORT
--------------------

Hutch customers can reach support 24 hours a day, 7 days a week on the Hutch Hotline at 1788. This is the primary number for Hutch customer service inquiries and issues.

Hutch also offers WhatsApp support for chat only (not voice calls) at 0788 777 111. Customers can also send an SMS to 5555, or email Hutch customer service at cs@hutchison.lk for account or plan-related questions.

Hutch customers can check their prepaid balance by dialing the USSD code star-three-four-four-hash (*344#), or their postpaid balance by dialing star-four-four-four-hash (*444#). Balances are also visible in the Hutch Self-Care App under the "My Usage" section.

Hutch's general USSD activation menu for many prepaid plans is star-one-three-one-hash (*131#). Postpaid customers activate or request plan changes mainly through the Hutch App, or by contacting the Hutch hotline at 1788, emailing cs@hutchison.lk, or visiting a Hutch shop.

HUTCH 15 — PREPAID NONSTOP SOCIAL MEDIA PLAN
---------------------------------------------

Hutch 15 is a prepaid nonstop data plan giving customers unlimited access to 15 popular apps without using their Anytime data: TikTok, Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, YouTube, ChatGPT, Snapchat, LinkedIn, Messenger, Viber, IMO, Telegram, X (formerly Twitter), Discord, and Gemini.

The Hutch 15 prepaid plan at Rs 1,199 runs for 30 days and includes 35GB of Anytime data, unlimited any-net calls, 500 any-net SMS, and a Rs 100 Freeload credited to a separate wallet. The nonstop social media benefit has a fair-use limit of 75GB before video quality is reduced.

The Hutch 15 prepaid plan at Rs 399 runs for 7 days and includes 9GB of Anytime data, unlimited any-net calls, 200 any-net SMS, and a Rs 25 Freeload. Its nonstop social media fair-use limit is 20GB.

There is also a Hutch 15 prepaid plan at Rs 358 for 30 days, which includes 2.5GB Anytime data, 300 any-net minutes, unlimited on-net minutes, 400 any-net SMS, and a Rs 40 cashback (not a Freeload).

Hutch 15 prepaid plans can be activated via Reload, the Hutch App, the Hutch website, or by dialing the USSD code *131#. Any Hutch prepaid or postpaid customer is eligible to activate a Hutch 15 plan, though only the Rs 1,199 plan is available to postpaid customers.

There are no daily limits on the Anytime data quota in Hutch 15 plans, and video streaming through the nonstop apps is capped at 360p resolution (240p after the fair-use limit is reached) since the plan is designed for smartphone use only.

HUTCH 15 — POSTPAID VERSION
-----------------------------

The Hutch 15 postpaid plan costs Rs 1,199 per month, discounted by the Rs 100 Freeload to an effective Rs 1,099 (including tax) per month, appearing as a line-item discount on the invoice. It includes 35GB Anytime data, unlimited any-net calls, 1GB of roaming data, and free news, sports and business alerts.

Hutch 15 postpaid can be activated online via the Hutch website or the Hutch App by any existing or new Hutch postpaid customer. Its nonstop social media fair-use limit is 75GB, after which video resolution steps down from 360p to 240p.

HOURLY / TIME-BASED UNLIMITED DATA PLANS
-------------------------------------------

Hutch offers three time-based unlimited data plans for prepaid customers only, activated exclusively through the Hutch App. These give completely unlimited data (no quota, no fair-use restriction) for a fixed number of hours from activation.

The Hutch hourly plans are: Rs 55 for 1 hour of unlimited data, Rs 95 for 2 hours of unlimited data, and Rs 185 for 5 hours of unlimited data — all prices inclusive of tax. Multiple hourly plans can be bought back-to-back, and their remaining time accumulates.

LEVEL-UP PLANS
----------------

Hutch Level-up plans give nonstop access to 11 apps without using Anytime data: Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, WhatsApp, Telegram, Viber, IMO, Messenger, LinkedIn, and X. Level-up comes in a "Data & Calls" version and a "Data Only" version.

Hutch Level-up at Rs 998 for 30 days includes 30GB Anytime data (no daily limit) and unlimited any-net calls, with nonstop access to the 11 Level-up apps; its fair-use limit is 20GB. Hutch Level-up at Rs 299 for 7 days includes 7GB Anytime data and unlimited any-net calls with the same nonstop apps; its fair-use limit is 7GB.

Hutch also offers Data-Only Level-up plans: Rs 699 for 30 days gives 3GB Anytime data plus nonstop access to the 11 apps (fair-use limit 20GB, no voice calls included), and Rs 209 for 7 days gives 1GB Anytime data plus the same nonstop apps (fair-use limit 7GB, no voice calls included).

Hutch Level-up plans can be activated by any prepaid or postpaid customer via Reload, the Hutch App, or USSD *131# for prepaid; postpaid customers can add it on or switch their base plan to Level-up via the Hutch App or by calling the Hutch contact centre.

ANY TIME DATA PLANS
----------------------

Hutch Any Time Data Plans give prepaid customers 100 percent of their data usable at any time of day, with no restrictions, activated via reload, USSD *131#, or the Hutch App.

The Hutch Any Time Data plan tiers are: 1GB for Rs 79 (7 days), 2GB for Rs 159 (14 days), 4GB for Rs 285 (30 days), 5.5GB for Rs 405 (30 days), 10.5GB for Rs 715 (30 days), 15.75GB for Rs 1,021 (30 days), and 21GB for Rs 1,228 (30 days). Excess usage beyond the quota is charged from other data balances or the main wallet.

MY PLAN — CUSTOMIZABLE PACK
------------------------------

Hutch My Plan is a fully customizable pack, available exclusively through the Hutch App, that lets a customer build their own bundle of Anytime data, Night Time data, Voice Any-net minutes, Voice On-net minutes, and SMS Any-net — choosing exactly the quota of each they want.

A Hutch My Plan pack starts from Rs 51 and has a validity of 30 days. Rates within My Plan are as low as 8 cents per MB for Anytime data and 2 cents per MB for Night Time data (usable 12am-9am), with local voice at Rs 1.50 plus tax per minute and local SMS at Rs 0.20 plus tax per SMS. Both prepaid and postpaid Hutch customers can create My Plan packs, and multiple packs can be activated at once, extending both quota and validity.

CLIQ — BREAK FREE INTERNET
------------------------------

Hutch cliQ is a time-based (not data-quota-based) internet service: customers pay for a duration rather than a data amount, and get nonstop internet access for that period. cliQ is available for both 078 and 072 Hutch numbers, on both prepaid and postpaid plans, and requires downloading the separate cliQ app (Android and iOS).

Hutch cliQ 4G Break Free plans (prices inclusive of tax, speeds up to 10 Mbps) are: 2 days for Rs 199, 4 days for Rs 341, 7 days for Rs 665, 10 days for Rs 765, 15 days for Rs 935, and 30 days for Rs 1,669. After a plan's fair-use data volume is reached, speed is reduced to around 1 Mbps for the remainder of the validity period rather than being cut off.

Multiple Hutch cliQ plans can be purchased and their remaining duration accumulates in the cliQ app. While a cliQ plan is active, standard "pay as you go" data charging from the main balance is disabled to prevent unexpected charges; any of Hutch's standard prepaid data plans can still be added on top when no cliQ plan is active.

WORK FROM HOME AND STUDY FROM HOME PLANS
--------------------------------------------

Hutch's Work From Home and Study From Home plans give discounted or restricted data access to a defined list of productivity and learning apps, aimed at remote work and online classes. The Hutch Study From Home Buddy Plan costs Rs 224 for 30 days and provides a 30GB Work From Home data quota, activated via USSD *131# for prepaid or *444# for postpaid.

The apps and sites covered by Hutch's Work From Home / Study From Home quota include Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Google Classroom, Gmail, Yahoo Mail, Microsoft Outlook, Google Search, Telegram, WhatsApp, IMO, Webex, and TRC-approved e-learning sites such as e-thaksalawa. YouTube access is only unlocked on Hutch's Rs 717 Work & Study From Home plan variant, and even then at 360p resolution only.

UNLIMITED DOWNLOADER — NIGHT TIME DATA
------------------------------------------

Hutch's Unlimited Downloader is a truly unlimited (no quota, no speed restriction) night-time data add-on, usable only between 12am and 8am, and available to prepaid customers only. It is ideal for heavy downloads, video streaming, or gaming overnight.

Hutch Unlimited Downloader plans are priced at Rs 62 for 1 night, Rs 117 for 3 nights, and Rs 236 for 7 nights, activated via USSD *131# or the Hutch App.

HUTCH 2X — MOBILE HOTSPOT AND HOME BROADBAND IN ONE
-------------------------------------------------------

Hutch 2X converts a customer's smartphone into a Wi-Fi hotspot to cover both mobile data and home broadband needs from one connection, with no router or installation costs, saving up to 60 percent compared to running separate connections. It is available to both prepaid and postpaid Hutch customers.

Hutch 2X plans, all with 30-day validity, are: 50GB for Rs 1,099, 100GB for Rs 2,099, 200GB for Rs 3,999, and a non-stop (unlimited) data option for Rs 4,999. On the unlimited Rs 4,999 Hutch 2X plan, speed steps down to a maximum of 5 Mbps after 250GB of usage, which still supports 1080p HD video streaming. Android hotspot devices support up to 10 connected devices; iOS supports up to 5.

ONE LOAD — ANNUAL PREPAID AND POSTPAID PLAN
-------------------------------------------------

Hutch OneLoad is Sri Lanka's annual telecom plan: customers recharge once and stay connected for 360 days (12 cycles of 30 days each), with unused monthly Anytime data and voice minutes rolling over into the next cycle up to a stated cap.

The Hutch OneLoad plan at Rs 2,499 per year gives an annual benefit of 120GB data and 6,000 voice minutes, broken down as 10GB Anytime data and 500 any-net minutes per 30-day cycle, plus nonstop WhatsApp; unused balance rolls over up to 30GB data and 1,000 minutes.

The Hutch OneLoad plan at Rs 8,999 per year gives 360GB data and unlimited voice annually, broken down as 30GB Anytime data per 30-day cycle with unlimited any-net calls and nonstop access to 9 social apps; unused data rolls over up to 60GB.

The Hutch OneLoad plan at Rs 14,999 per year gives nonstop (nonstop-quality) data and unlimited voice every cycle, with no rollover cap stated since the data benefit is already nonstop.

Hutch OneLoad plans can be activated by any Hutch customer: prepaid customers add it on by reloading the exact plan price, the Hutch App, or USSD *131#; existing postpaid customers request a package change via the Hutch App, email, Hutch.lk chat, or the 1788 contact centre. A OneLoad money-back guarantee applies only to a brand-new connection activated with OneLoad: the refund request must be made within 7 days of SIM activation by sending an SMS to 8800 with the Hutch number, NIC, and an alternate number, provided usage stayed at or below 2GB data, 30 minutes of calls, and 30 SMS.

GAURAWA PLAN — GOVERNMENT, PENSIONER AND CLERGY PLAN
----------------------------------------------------------

The Hutch Gaurawa Plan is an exclusive package for government sector employees, pensioners, government-certified journalists, and clergy, available on both prepaid and postpaid connections, subject to submitting eligibility documents (NIC/driving license/passport plus a staff ID, pensioner ID, journalist registration, or denomination ID as applicable).

Hutch Gaurawa Plan tiers (30-day validity, unlimited any-net calls on all of them) are: Rs 342 for 5GB Anytime data and 300 any-net SMS, Rs 549 for 15GB Anytime data and 1,000 any-net SMS, and Rs 779 for 30GB Anytime data and 1,000 any-net SMS.

A new Hutch Gaurawa SIM can be ordered with doorstep delivery at store.hutch.lk/gaurawa. Existing Hutch customers who qualify can switch to Gaurawa by sending the required documents via WhatsApp to 078 601 0161, or by submitting a request letter and documents to the dedicated Gaurawa WhatsApp number +94 78 700 0541.

UNLIMITED ANY NET CALLS PLAN
--------------------------------

The Hutch Unlimited Any Net Calls plan at Rs 599 for 30 days is available to both prepaid and postpaid customers, and includes unlimited any-net voice calling, 500 any-net SMS, and 3GB Anytime data.

The Hutch Unlimited Any Net Calls plan at Rs 189 for 7 days is available to prepaid customers only, and includes unlimited any-net voice calling, 125 any-net SMS, and 1GB Anytime data. Both tiers activate via the Hutch App, USSD *141#, or by recharging the exact pack value.

HUTCH ULTIMATE — NONSTOP DATA PLUS UNLIMITED CALLS
--------------------------------------------------------

Hutch Ultimate combines nonstop (effectively unlimited) Anytime data with unlimited any-net calling. The Rs 1,999 Hutch Ultimate plan (30 days, both prepaid and postpaid) includes nonstop data with a 100GB fair-use threshold before speed steps down to 1 Mbps, unlimited any-net calls, 500 any-net SMS, and a Rs 100 Freeload.

The Rs 619 Hutch Ultimate plan (7 days, prepaid only) includes nonstop data with a 25GB fair-use threshold before speed steps down to 1 Mbps, unlimited any-net calls, 125 any-net SMS, and a Rs 25 Freeload. Both Hutch Ultimate tiers can be activated via reload, the Hutch App, or USSD *131# for prepaid; postpaid customers activate via the Hutch App.

POSTPAID COMBO PLANS
------------------------

Hutch Postpaid Combo plans bundle nonstop data, unlimited social media, and unlimited any-net calls for postpaid customers. The Rs 1,999 Hutch Postpaid Combo plan (30 days) includes unlimited any-net calls, 500 any-net SMS, nonstop Anytime data, and a Rs 100 Freeload.

The Rs 998 Hutch Postpaid Combo plan (30 days, the Level-up postpaid tier) includes nonstop access to 11 social media apps, unlimited any-net calls, 30GB Anytime data with no daily limits, 1GB of free roaming data, and free sports, business and news alerts.

The Rs 599 Hutch Postpaid Combo plan (30 days) includes unlimited any-net calls, 500 any-net SMS, and 3GB Anytime data. Usage beyond any Hutch Postpaid Combo bundle is charged at Rs 0.25 per SMS, Rs 1.50 per minute of calls, and Rs 0.20 per MB of data, with speed limits of 10 Mbps uplink, up to 20 Mbps downlink on 4G, and up to 7.2 Mbps downlink on 3G.

STANDARD RATES (OUTSIDE ANY BUNDLE)
---------------------------------------

Outside any Hutch bundle or add-on plan, Hutch's standard local call rate is Rs 0.03 per second on any network, and the standard local SMS rate is Rs 0.25 per SMS on any network. These are the base "pay as you go" rates that apply when a customer has no active voice/SMS bundle.

Hutch's standard off-peak data rate (outside an active data plan) is Rs 0.30 per MB between midnight and 6pm, and Rs 0.60 per MB between 6pm and midnight. Customers are always better off activating one of Hutch's data plans (such as Any Time Data or My Plan) than relying on these standard pay-as-you-go data rates.

GENERAL POLICIES
-------------------

Almost all Hutch data plans described here — Hutch 15, Level-up, cliQ, hourly plans, Unlimited Downloader, and similar nonstop or unlimited offers — are designed and intended for smartphone use only, and are not intended for use on routers, modems, or other non-smartphone devices, though many can still work over a phone's own mobile hotspot.

Hutch plans marked "Nonstop" or "Unlimited" for data are still subject to a Fair Usage Policy (FUP) in most cases: once a stated data volume is reached within the validity period, video or overall speed is reduced (commonly to around 1 Mbps) for the rest of that period, rather than the connection being cut off. Genuinely unlimited exceptions (such as the Hutch hourly plans and Unlimited Downloader night-time plans) are called out explicitly as having no FUP.

Hutch plan prices, data allowances, and terms mentioned here are subject to periodic review by Hutch and may be revised in line with market trends and Telecommunications Regulatory Commission (TRC) approvals; a caller should be told current details are confirmed via the Hutch App, Hutch.lk, or the Hutch hotline at 1788 if precision is critical.
