HORIZON AIRLINE AND AVIATION ACADEMY KNOWLEDGE BASE
====================================================

Horizon Airline and Aviation Academy is a private aviation training college in Sri Lanka, based in the heart of Colombo, that commenced academic programs in October 2015. Horizon Airline and Aviation Academy is authorized and monitored by the Tertiary and Vocational Education Commission (TVEC) of Sri Lanka, under the Ministry of Skills Development, to provide vocational training in Airline and Aviation studies. Horizon crafts professionals for careers as Airline Cabin Crew, in Cargo and Logistics, and in Ground Operations, to global standards.

Horizon Airline and Aviation Academy is strategically located close to twelve leading national schools and serves a catchment of about twenty thousand potential students. Horizon has enrolled foreign students from India, the Maldives, and the Philippines. Horizon has been recognized as a leading airline training college in Sri Lanka and is a twice-winner of the "Best Airline Training College in Sri Lanka" award, in the 2020 to 2021 period. Horizon has operated since 2015, empowering the next generation of aviation professionals.

VISION AND MISSION
------------------

Horizon Airline and Aviation Academy's vision is to be the premier source for education, a unique workforce training institute, and to create skilled and value-based resource professionals for the aviation industry.

Horizon Airline and Aviation Academy's mission is to nurture and transform competent students into confident and capable candidates ready to take up challenging careers in the aviation industry by providing quality education.

OUR OBJECTIVES
--------------

Horizon Airline and Aviation Academy exists to create clear academic pathways: the academy bridges the gap for school leavers who otherwise have no clear route into the fast-growing airline industry. Since 2015, Horizon's primary objective has been to provide in-house training and the support youth need to secure job placements with leading airlines, leisure agents, and freight forwarders. Horizon is committed to excellence, using both traditional and innovative teaching methods to help students reach their academic, personal, and professional potential at an affordable price.

ALL FOUR DIPLOMAS AT A GLANCE
-----------------------------

Horizon Airline and Aviation Academy offers four diploma programs: the Diploma in Airline Cabin Crew, the Diploma in Airport Ground Operations, the Diploma in Airline Ticketing Reservations and Marketing, and the Diploma in Air Cargo and Logistics. All four diplomas run for six to eight months, available part-time or full-time on flexible weekday and weekend schedules. The Diploma in Airline Cabin Crew costs one hundred thousand Sri Lankan rupees, that is LKR 100,000; the other three diplomas cost eighty-five thousand rupees, that is LKR 85,000 each. Every diploma requires six passes at the G.C.E. Ordinary Level examination including Maths and English, and the Airline Cabin Crew diploma additionally requires a C pass in Maths and English and a minimum arms reach height of 212 centimetres.

DIPLOMA IN AIRLINE CABIN CREW
-----------------------------

The Horizon Diploma in Airline Cabin Crew is a comprehensive airline diploma covering passenger safety, service excellence, and professional grooming to prepare students for a global cabin crew career. The course fee for the Horizon Diploma in Airline Cabin Crew is one hundred thousand Sri Lankan rupees, that is LKR 100,000.

The Horizon Diploma in Airline Cabin Crew runs for six to eight months and is available part-time or full-time, with flexible weekday and weekend class schedules. The entry requirement is six passes at the G.C.E. Ordinary Level examination including Maths and English at a C pass, and a minimum arms reach height of 212 centimetres. Students are evaluated through modularised closed-book written examinations.

The Horizon Diploma in Airline Cabin Crew syllabus covers cabin crew roles and responsibilities, crew coordination and communication, handling passengers with special needs, food and beverages served on board, safety and emergency procedures, first aid and medical emergencies, and Dangerous Goods control. Practical training includes industry field visits, water survival and fire drill training, grooming and deportment, an Interview Ready Program, fitness training, and career guidance and support.

DIPLOMA IN AIRPORT GROUND OPERATIONS
------------------------------------

The Horizon Diploma in Airport Ground Operations teaches the essential skills for airport operations, from passenger handling and travel planning to air cargo and aviation safety regulations. The course fee for the Horizon Diploma in Airport Ground Operations is eighty-five thousand Sri Lankan rupees, that is LKR 85,000.

The Horizon Diploma in Airport Ground Operations runs for six to eight months, part-time or full-time, with flexible weekday and weekend schedules. The entry requirement is six passes at the G.C.E. Ordinary Level examination including Maths and English. Students are evaluated through a final open-book written examination and assignments.

The Horizon Diploma in Airport Ground Operations syllabus covers travel information and planning, passenger services, customer services, regulatory bodies and air traffic services, introduction to air cargo, weight and balance, aircraft types and loading, aircraft operational messages, aviation weather, and DGR awareness. It also includes industry field visits, grooming and deportment, an Interview Ready Program, and career guidance and support.

DIPLOMA IN AIRLINE TICKETING, RESERVATIONS AND MARKETING
--------------------------------------------------------

The Horizon Diploma in Airline Ticketing, Reservations and Marketing gives expert knowledge in IATA geography, fare calculations, GDS systems, and airline marketing to excel in the travel and aviation industry. The course fee for the Horizon Diploma in Airline Ticketing, Reservations and Marketing is eighty-five thousand Sri Lankan rupees, that is LKR 85,000.

The Horizon Diploma in Airline Ticketing, Reservations and Marketing runs for six to eight months, part-time or full-time, with flexible weekday and weekend schedules. The entry requirement is six passes at the G.C.E. Ordinary Level examination including Maths and English. Students are evaluated through a final closed-book written examination and assignments.

The Horizon Diploma in Airline Ticketing, Reservations and Marketing syllabus covers IATA world airline geography, fares and ticketing calculations, sales and marketing techniques, travel agency back-office functions, and effective communication and sales skills, with optional GDS training. It also includes industry field visits, grooming and deportment, an Interview Ready Program, and career guidance and support.

DIPLOMA IN AIR CARGO AND LOGISTICS
----------------------------------

The Horizon Diploma in Air Cargo and Logistics develops a strong foundation in the air cargo industry, covering aircraft structures, cargo acceptance, freight forwarding procedures, and logistics management. The course fee for the Horizon Diploma in Air Cargo and Logistics is eighty-five thousand Sri Lankan rupees, that is LKR 85,000.

The Horizon Diploma in Air Cargo and Logistics runs for six to eight months, part-time or full-time, with flexible weekday and weekend schedules. The entry requirement is six passes at the G.C.E. Ordinary Level examination including Maths and English. Students are evaluated through a final open-book written examination and assignments.

The Horizon Diploma in Air Cargo and Logistics syllabus covers an introduction to the air cargo industry, aircraft structures and parts, general cargo acceptance principles, air cargo rates and charges calculations, floor loading limitations, freight forwarding procedures, e-freight, introduction to logistics, and DGR awareness. It also includes industry field visits, grooming and deportment, an Interview Ready Program, and career guidance and support.
