IAAC — INTERNATIONAL AIRLINE AND AVIATION COLLEGE KNOWLEDGE BASE
================================================================

IAAC, the International Airline and Aviation College, is a private aviation training institute in Sri Lanka that commenced academic programs in October 2015. IAAC is strategically located in the heart of Colombo and is authorized and monitored by the Tertiary and Vocational Education Commission (TVEC) of Sri Lanka, under the Ministry of Skills Development, to provide vocational training in Airline and Aviation studies. IAAC's tagline is that it is the best airline training college in Asia.

IAAC crafts professionals for careers as Airline Cabin Crew, in Cargo and Logistics, and in Ground Operations, to global standards. IAAC is close to twelve leading national schools and caters to about twenty thousand potential students. IAAC has enrolled foreign students from India, the Maldives, and the Philippines. IAAC was the recipient of the "Best Airline Training College" in Sri Lanka award, awarded twice in 2020 and 2021.

IAAC has three academies in Sri Lanka. The IAAC City Academy is in Colombo 10. The IAAC Airport Academy is at Ratmalana Airport. The IAAC Central Academy is in Kurunegala. IAAC has operated since 2015 as Sri Lanka's premier aviation institute, empowering the next generation of aviation professionals.

DIPLOMA IN AIRLINE CABIN CREW
-----------------------------

The IAAC Diploma in Airline Cabin Crew is a comprehensive international airline diploma covering passenger safety, service excellence, and professional grooming to prepare students for a global cabin crew career. The course fee for the IAAC Diploma in Airline Cabin Crew at the City Campus is one hundred thousand Sri Lankan rupees, that is LKR 100,000.

The IAAC Diploma in Airline Cabin Crew runs for six to eight months and is available part-time or full-time, with flexible weekday and weekend class schedules. The entry requirement is six passes at the G.C.E. Ordinary Level examination including Maths and English at a C pass, and a minimum arms reach height of 212 centimetres. Students are evaluated through modularised closed-book written examinations.

The IAAC Diploma in Airline Cabin Crew syllabus covers cabin crew roles and responsibilities, crew coordination and communication, handling passengers with special needs, food and beverages served on board, safety and emergency procedures, first aid and medical emergencies, and Dangerous Goods control. Practical training includes industry field visits, water survival and fire drill training, grooming and deportment, an Interview Ready Program, BMI and general fitness training, and career guidance and support.

DIPLOMA IN AIRPORT GROUND OPERATIONS
------------------------------------

The IAAC Diploma in Airport Ground Operations teaches the essential skills for airport operations, from passenger handling and travel planning to air cargo and aviation safety regulations. The course fee for the IAAC Diploma in Airport Ground Operations at the City Campus is eighty-five thousand Sri Lankan rupees, that is LKR 85,000.

The IAAC Diploma in Airport Ground Operations runs for six to eight months, part-time or full-time, with flexible weekday and weekend schedules. The entry requirement is six passes at the G.C.E. Ordinary Level examination including Maths and English. Students are evaluated through a final open-book written examination and assignments.

The IAAC Diploma in Airport Ground Operations syllabus covers travel information and planning, passenger services, customer services, regulatory bodies and air traffic services, introduction to air cargo, weight and balance, aircraft types and loading, aircraft operational messages, aviation weather, and DGR awareness. It also includes industry field visits, grooming and deportment, an Interview Ready Program, and career guidance and support.

The course fee for the IAAC Diploma in Airline Ticketing, Reservations and Marketing at the City Campus is eighty-five thousand Sri Lankan rupees, that is LKR 85,000.

The IAAC Diploma in Airline Ticketing, Reservations and Marketing runs for six to eight months, part-time or full-time, with flexible weekday and weekend schedules. The entry requirement is six passes at the G.C.E. Ordinary Level examination including Maths and English. Students are evaluated through a final closed-book written examination and assignments.

The IAAC Diploma in Airline Ticketing, Reservations and Marketing syllabus covers IATA world airline geography, fares and ticketing calculations, sales and marketing techniques, travel agency back-office functions, and effective communication and sales skills, with optional GDS training. It also includes industry field visits, grooming and deportment, an Interview Ready Program, and career guidance and support.

DIPLOMA IN AIR CARGO AND LOGISTICS
----------------------------------

The IAAC Diploma in Air Cargo and Logistics develops a strong foundation in the air cargo industry, covering aircraft structures, cargo acceptance, freight forwarding procedures, and logistics management. The course fee for the IAAC Diploma in Air Cargo and Logistics at the City Campus is eighty-five thousand Sri Lankan rupees, that is LKR 85,000.

The IAAC Diploma in Air Cargo and Logistics runs for six to eight months, part-time or full-time, with flexible weekday and weekend schedules. The entry requirement is six passes at the G.C.E. The IAAC Diploma in Air Cargo and Logistics syllabus covers an introduction to the air cargo industry, aircraft structures and parts, general cargo acceptance principles, air cargo rates and charges calculations, floor loading limitations, freight forwarding procedures, e-freight, introduction to logistics, and DGR awareness. It also includes industry field visits, grooming and deportment, an Interview Ready Program, and career guidance and support.
