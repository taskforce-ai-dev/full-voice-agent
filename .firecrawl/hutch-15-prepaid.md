## Frequently asked questions

 Who can activate Hutch 15 plans? 

 Any Hutch Prepaid or Postpaid customer can activate a Hutch 15 plan. 

 How can I activate Hutch 15 plans? 

**If Prepaid**

You can activate the respective plan of your choice via Reload, Hutch APP, Web, USSD Menu – *131#

**If Postpaid**

Only the Rs. 1199 plan is available for postpaid customers, and it can be activated via the Hutch App.

 Do I receive the Freeload as money or discount for the package price? 

**If Prepaid**

Your Freeload will be credited to a separate wallet linked to your Hutch connection. You can use this Freeload to purchase any Hutch local product or service, but it will not be applied as a direct discount on the package price.

**If Postpaid**

The postpaid freeload will appear under the “Free/Units/Currency – Detail” Description section of your invoice as “FREELOAD – Rs. 100”

With this freeload applied, you will be charged only Rs. 1,099 (Incl. Tax) per month.

 What is the validity period of the Freeload? 

| Plan | Freeload Validity |
| --- | --- |
| Rs. 1199 | 30 Days |
| Rs. 399 | 7 Days |

 What are the social media benefits of Hutch 15 plans? 

The standout feature of the Hutch 15 plans is nonstop access to a wide range of popular social media platforms,

including: TikTok,YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, Messenger, Viber, IMO, Telegram, LinkedIn, X (formerly Twitter), Snapchat, Discode as well as access to leading AI platforms like Gemini and ChatGPT – all without consuming your Anytime data!”

 Does the Social media benefit have any limits? 

 These plans are designed to offer a nonstop experience to smartphone user. However, to ensure a fair user experience amongst all users, who generate high usage beyond a reasonable point will be subjected to Video quality degradation for the remaining validity period of the plan. 
| Plan | Freeload Validity | FUP Limit Applicable @ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rs. 1199 | 30 Days | 75GB |
| Rs. 399 | 7 Days | 20GB |

 Can I use my Anytime data quota without any daily limits ? 

 YES, you can! There are NO DAILY LIMITS for the Anytime data quota of these plans. 

 What happens when my Anytime Data quota is exhausted before the pack expiry? 

 YES, you can! There are NO DAILY LIMITS for the Anytime data quota of these plans. 

 Can I use these content through app and browser both? 

 You can continue to enjoy the Nonstop 15 experience. For any other usage, the data will be charged from your available data balances or your main wallet balance. 

 What video quality can I expect when using this plan, and what happens after I reach the FUP limit ? 

 These plans are designed to offer you an Non-Stop video streaming at maximum video resolution of 360p for TikTok, Instagram, YouTube , Facebook. After reaching FUP, the maximum video resolution will be reduced to 240p 

 What usage on Nonstop Apps would be considered for Nonstop usage? 

 Services hosted directly on respective Nonstop Apps servers would qualify for Nonstop usage on this plan. For instance, Voice calls, Video calls, and chat services would be eligible for Nonstop data. However, if a customer attempts to access a link received through respective Nonstop Apps that leads to content hosted outside of the respective Nonstop Apps platform, the usage will be deducted from other data balances or main wallet balances. 

 Is this plan compatible with all devices? 

 This plan is exclusively intended for smartphone use.
