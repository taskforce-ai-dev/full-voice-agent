## Diploma in Airline Cabin Crew

A comprehensive International Airline Diploma covering passenger safety, service excellence, and professional grooming to prepare you for a global cabin crew career.

## Course Syllabus & Content

Theory
*   Cabin crew members' roles and responsibilities.
*   Crew member coordination and communication.
*   Handling of Passengers with special needs.
*   Food and beverages served on board.
*   Safety and emergency procedures.
*   First aid and handling medical emergencies.
*   Dangerous Goods Control.

Practical
*   Industry Relevant Field Visits.
*   Personal water survival/fire drill training.
*   Grooming and Deportment training.
*   Interview Ready Program.
*   BMI maintenance/general fitness training.
*   Career guidance and support.
