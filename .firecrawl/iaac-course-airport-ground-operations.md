## Diploma in Airport Ground Operations

Master the essential skills for airport operations, from passenger handling and travel planning to air cargo and aviation safety regulations.

## Course Syllabus & Content

*   Travel Information and Planning.
*   Passenger Services.
*   Customer Services.
*   Regulatory Bodies and Air Traffic Services.
*   Introduction to Air Cargo.
*   Introduction to Weight and Balance.
*   Aircraft Types and Loading.
*   Aircraft Operational Messages.
*   Aviation Weather.
*   DGR Awareness.
*   Industry-Related Field Visits
*   Grooming and Deportment Training
*   Interview Ready Program
*   Career guidance and support
