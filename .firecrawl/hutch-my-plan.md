My Plan is exclusively available via HUTCH app. It gives you absolute flexibility to design and activate packs of your choice without being limited to predefined packages.

 To access Hutch My Plan & create your pack, download the Hutch app from Playstore/Appstore or Huwawei App gallery.

TRC/H/PRO/21/03

## What is special about Hutch My Plan

➤ My Plan gives the complete convenience and flexibility to determine the amount of data, voice and sms needed to create your own pack with the available credit balance in your account.

➤ You can create packs by selecting combinations from Hutch to Any Network Minutes only, Hutch to Any network SMS only , Anytime data only, Night Time data only or a combinations of any of them according to your preference.

➤ Both prepaid & postpaid customers can create packs.

## Rates & Validity

➤ The bigger the quota of the pack you create the cheaper the package gets.

➤ Provides data rates as low as 08 cents per MB for Anytime data and 02 cents per MB for Night time data.

➤ Local Voice rates 1.50 + taxes per minute

➤ Local SMS rates 0.20 cents + taxes per sms

➤ Validity of 30 days

## FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

*   [1. How can I check the My Plan package available Data/ Minutes and SMS?](javascript:void(0))Simply visit the Hutch app “My Usage” section – Data/Voice or SMS tab which will display the quota under the names anytime data (My Plan), Night time data (My Plan), Anynet Voice (My Plan) and Anynet SMS (My Plan).  
*   [2. Can I use the Anytime & Night time quota packs on both 3G & 4G network?](javascript:void(0))Yes, it can be used on both 3G and 4G networks. 
*   [3. Are the packs created using My Plan auto renewal?](javascript:void(0))No. The packs activated are one-time add on packs.  

*   [4. What are the time bands I can use the Anytime Data & Night time data quotas?](javascript:void(0))Night time data - 00:00 am – 09:00 am hours Anytime data – Usage 24Hrs/day 
*   [5. Can I activate multiple My Plan packs?](javascript:void(0))Yes, you can activate multiple My Plan packs. And the data quota and validity will be extended. 
*   [6. Can I use Data Quota Plans such as Anytime or Cliq while I have a My Plan?](javascript:void(0))Yes, you can. 
*   [7. Can this plan be used on different Devices ?](javascript:void(0))This plan is exclusively intended for smartphone use.
