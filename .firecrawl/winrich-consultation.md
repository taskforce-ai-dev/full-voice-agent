# Consultation & Equipment  Selection

![](https://winrich.lk/storage/uploads/images/original/1768803837_696dcdfd8dc8b.png)[Request a Consultation](https://winrich.lk/contact-us)

The right equipment plan starts with understanding your menu, space and capacity.

Before you invest in any machine, our consultants work with you to understand how
your operation really runs – your menu, production volumes, workflow, staffing and
available space. Based on this, we design an equipment lineup that is practical,
scalable and realistic for your kitchen or bakery.
From ovens and mixers to steamers, prep and support equipment, every
recommendation is made with efficiency, reliability and total cost of ownership in mind

###### What we offer

- Site assessments and workflow planning
- Capacity calculations and equipment sizing
- Options based on budget, energy use and future expansion
- Guidance on certified global brands, models and configurations

###### Why Choose Our Service?

- Reduced risk of equipment damage or voided warranties
- Maximized equipment efficiency and lifespan
- Ensured staff safety and operational confidence
- Seamless integration into your daily workflow

###### Result

A clear, tailored equipment plan you can trust — matched to your space and production
needs, and ready to move straight into installation and commissioning.
