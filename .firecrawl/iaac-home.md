## Welcome to IAAC

International Airline and Aviation College (IAAC) is a private educational institute based in Sri Lanka, strategically located in the heart of Colombo. We are authorized and monitored by the **TVEC** (Tertiary and Vocational Education Commission) under the Ministry of Skills Development.

We cater to about 20,000 potential students near 12 leading national schools, providing top-tier vocational training in Airline and Aviation studies.

[Intro video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0X1SG86awVM)

## Our Specialized Training

We offer specialized training courses designed to meet the future employment needs of the aviation industry.

### Airline Cabin Crew
Comprehensive training for aspiring flight attendants focusing on safety, grooming, and in-flight service.

### Airport Ground Operations
Mastering passenger handling, check-in procedures, terminal operations, and ramp safety.

### Ticketing & Reservations
Expert training in global airline ticketing systems (GDS), reservations, and travel management.

### Cargo & Logistics
Specialized courses in air cargo operations, supply chain management, and global logistics.
