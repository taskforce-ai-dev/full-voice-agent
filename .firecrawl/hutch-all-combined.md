## Frequently asked questions

 1. What are Hutch time-based unlimited data plans? 

 Hutch Time-based Unlimited Data Plans are time-based plans that provide unlimited data access for the selected validity period. 

 2. How do time-based unlimited data plans work? 

 Once you activate a time-based unlimited data plan, you get unlimited data access for the plan’s validity period. The plan automatically expires when the validity period ends. 

 3. What time-based unlimited data plans are available and what are the prices? 

 Hutch offers three Hourly Plan options: 
| Plan | Price (Rs.) | Validity (Hours) | Data Limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Plan 1 | Rs. 55 | 1 hour | Unlimited |
| Plan 2 | Rs. 95 | 2 hours | Unlimited |
| Plan 3 | Rs. 185 | 5 hours | Unlimited |
 All prices are inclusive of taxes. 

 4. Who can activate a time-based unlimited data plan? 

 These plans are available only for prepaid customers. 

 5. How do I activate a time-based unlimited data plan? 

 You can activate a time-based unlimited data plan through the Hutch App only. 

 6. Can I buy multiple time-based unlimited data plans at once? 

 Yes. You can activate multiple time-based plans back-to-back. Each plan is valid from the time of activation and will run for its own validity period separately. 

 7. Can I use time-based unlimited data plans for streaming and video? 

 Absolutely! Time-based unlimited data plans provide unlimited data during their validity period, so you can browse, stream, download, and scroll without extra charges—within the plan time frame. 

 8. What happens when the plan expires? 

 Once the validity ends, your internet will revert to standard pay-as-you-go data rates (if available) or your next active data pack. 

 9. Is there any fair usage policy? 

 No. There is no Fair Usage Policy (FUP) applied to the Hourly Plans. You will enjoy unlimited data access throughout the full validity period of the selected plan. 

 10. Can time-based unlimited data plans be used on all devices? 

 This plan is exclusively intended for smartphone use.

## Frequently asked questions

[1. Who can activate Level-up plans?](https://hutch.lk/levelup/)

Any Hutch Prepaid or Postpaid customer can activate a Level-up plan.

[2. How can I activate Level-up plans?](https://hutch.lk/levelup/)

**If Prepaid**

You can activate the respective plan as add-on of your choice via Reload, Hutch APP, USSD Menu – *131#.

**If Postpaid**

You can activate the plan as a recurring or one-time add on via Hutch app.

If you wish to change you monthly base plan to Level-up Rs 998 plan, then you may request a package change via Hutch App or by Calling our contact centre.

[3.What are the social media benefits of Level-up plans?](https://hutch.lk/levelup/)

The speciality of this plan is that it offers you the opportunity to upgrade your life by having nonstop access to special apps like Linkedin & X (Formerly twitter) along with the popular social media applications such as; YouTube, FB, Instagram, TikTok, Messenger, WhatsApp, Viber, IMO & Telegram. 

[4.Can I use total Anytime Data quota all at once?](https://hutch.lk/levelup/)

YES, you can! There are NO DAILY LIMITS for the Anytime data quota of these plans. 

[5. Does the Social media benefit have any limits?](https://hutch.lk/levelup/)

These plans are designed to offer a truly nonstop experience to smartphone user.

However, to ensure a fair user experience amongst all users, users who generate high usage beyond a reasonable point will be subjected to Video quality degradation for the remaining validity period of the plan.

[6. Can I use these content through app & browser both?](https://hutch.lk/levelup/)

Yes, there is no restrictions on the platform used to access these content offered on this plan. However please note when accessing via browser you may use more data & also burn more background data. 

[7. What happens when my Anytime Data quota is exhausted before the pack expiry?](https://hutch.lk/levelup/)

You can continue to enjoy the Nonstop 11 experience. For any other usage, the data will be charged from your available data balances or your main wallet balance.

[8. Can I watch HD quality videos on this plan?](https://hutch.lk/levelup/)

These plans are designed to offer you an Non-Stop video streaming at maximum video resolution of 260p for TikTok, Instagram, YouTube & Facebook.

[9. How can I check my Balance ?](https://hutch.lk/levelup/)

You can check your balance form Hutch Self-care App. 

[10. What usage on Telegram would be considered for Nonstop usage?](https://hutch.lk/levelup/)

Services hosted directly on Telegram servers would qualify for Nonstop usage on this plan. For instance, Voice calls, Video calls, and chat services would be eligible for Nonstop data. However, if a customer attempts to access a link received through Telegram that leads to content hosted outside of the Telegram platform, the usage will be deducted from other data balances or main wallet balances. 

[11. Can this plan be used on different Devices ?](https://hutch.lk/levelup/)

This plan is exclusively intended for smartphone use.
