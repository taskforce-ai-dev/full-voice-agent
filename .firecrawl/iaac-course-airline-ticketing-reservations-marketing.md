Gain expert knowledge in IATA geography, fare calculations, GDS systems, and airline marketing strategies to excel in the travel and aviation industry.

## Course Syllabus & Content

*   IATA world Airline Geography
*   Fares & Ticketing calculations
*   Sales & Marketing Techniques
*   Travel Agency Back-office functions
*   Effective communication/Sale and Marketing Skills
*   Optional : GDS training
*   Industry Relevant Field Visits
*   Grooming and Deportment training
*   Interview Ready Program.
*   Career guidance and Support.
