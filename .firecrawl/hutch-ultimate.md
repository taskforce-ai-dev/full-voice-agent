*   [2. Is this plan available for both prepaid and postpaid users ?](javascript:void(0))The monthly plan is available for both prepaid and postpaid customers, while the weekly plan is available for prepaid customers only. 
*   [3. How to activate these plans ?](javascript:void(0))For Prepaid : You can activate your preferred plan by reloading the exact amount or through the Hutch App or by dialing *131# Postpaid: You can activate your preferred plan via the Hutch App.  
*   [4. What does “Non-Stop Anytime Data” mean?](javascript:void(0))You get continuous data 24/7 during the plan validity, with no time restrictions.  

*   [5. What happens when FUP limit is reached ?](javascript:void(0))Once you reach the data limit, speeds will be reduced to 1Mbps, while data usage remains uninterrupted. 
*   [6. How does the Freeload work?](javascript:void(0))The respective Freeload amount will be credited to your wallet. Customers can use this Freeload for VAS services or to subscribe to any other plan.  
*   [7. Can this plan be used in different devices?](javascript:void(0))These plans are exclusively intended for smartphone use.
