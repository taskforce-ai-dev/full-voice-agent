## Diploma in Air Cargo & Logistics

Develop a strong foundation in the air cargo industry, covering aircraft structures, cargo acceptance, freight forwarding procedures, and logistics management.

**Course Fee**
### LKR 85,000
Selected branch: IAAC City Campus

**Course Duration**
### 06 to 08 Months (Part Time)
Part-Time / Full-Time

**Entry Requirements**
06 Passes at G.C.E O/L Exam inclusive of Maths and English.

## Course Syllabus & Content

*   Introduction to the Air Cargo Industry
*   Aircraft Structures and Parts
*   General Cargo Acceptance Principles
*   Air Cargo Rates / Charges Calculations
*   Floor Loading Limitations
*   Freight Forwarding Procedures
*   E-freight
*   Introduction to Logistics
*   DGR Awareness
*   Industry Relevant Field Visits
*   Grooming and Deportment Training
*   Interview Ready Program
*   Career Guidance and Support
