## Required documents

## Government Employees:

*   National Identity Card or Driving License or Valid Passport
*   Staff ID issued from the institution, OR Confirmation of service by way of letter issued by relevant institution.

## Government Registered Journalists:

*   National Identity Card or Driving License or Valid Passport
*   Proof of registration as Government Information Department registered journalist

## Pensioners

*    National Identity Card or Driving License or Valid Passport
*    Pensioners Identity Card

## Clergy

*   National Identity Card or Driving License or Valid Passport
*   Identity Card or letter from the relevant denomination

## Frequently asked questions

[1. Who can get a Gaurawa plan ?](https://hutch.lk/gaurawa-plan)

Gaurawa is a special plan offered to all Government employees, Registered journalists, Pensioners, and Clergy

[2. Are these plans available on both prepaid and postpaid ?](https://hutch.lk/gaurawa-plan)

Yes ,they are available for both the prepaid and postpaid customers

[3. What are the supporting documents required to get this special plan ?](https://hutch.lk/gaurawa-plan)

**For Government Employees:**

1. National Identity Card or Driving License or Valid Passport

2. Staff ID issued from the institution, OR Confirmation of service by way of letter issued by relevant institution.

**For Journalists (registered to the Government Information Department)**

1. National Identity Card or Driving License or Valid Passport

2. Proof of registration as Government Information Department registered journalist

**For Pensioners**

1. National Identity Card or Driving License or Valid Passport

2. Pensioners Identity Card

**For Clergy**

1. National Identity Card or Driving License or Valid Passport

2. Respective denomination Identity Card or a letter in a letter head

[4. Can I activate Gaurawa special plan on my existing Hutch number ?](https://hutch.lk/gaurawa-plan)

You cannot activate Gaurawa plans on your standard Hutch connection. If you wish to activate Gaurawa plans on your same number you need to request for a package change.

Steps to convert your package;

Submit a request letter and the necessary documentation listed in question #3. The required documents can be shared via [WhatsApp (0787000541)](https://wa.me/94787000541) or email [cs@hutchison.lk](mailto:cs@hutchison.lk)

[5. How can I activate Gaurawa if I'm a new Hutch customer?](https://hutch.lk/gaurawa-plan)

If you meet the eligibility criteria mentioned in question #1,

1. You can easily order your new Gaurawa SIM by doorstep delivery clicking here: [https://store.hutch.lk/gaurawa](https://store.hutch.lk/gaurawa)

OR

2. Visit one of our Hutch shops or dedicated Gaurawa retailer near you.

[6. Can I switch to a different plan after activating Gaurawa?](https://hutch.lk/gaurawa-plan)

Yes, If you are a Prepaid customer you may activate any add-on plan after the expiry of the Gaurawa Package.

If you are a Postpaid customer you can submit the package change request via Hutch App/ email : [cs@hutchison.lk](mailto:cs@hutchison.lk)

[7. How do I check my Gaurawa Plan balance?](https://hutch.lk/gaurawa-plan)

The Hutch App allows you to easily monitor your Gaurawa plan balance. [http://onelink.to/qf6rj4](http://onelink.to/qf6rj4)

[8. How do I activate/renew a Gaurawa plan on my prepaid connection ?](https://hutch.lk/gaurawa-plan)

If you already have a Gaurawa package, You may Reload the exact pack value, Visit Hutch app or Dial *393#

[9. What if I finish my Data & SMS quota allocated for the plan ?](https://hutch.lk/gaurawa-plan)

You can continue browsing data. However, accessing other content will be charged using your Anytime data balance or credit balance. If more data required, purchase an Anytime data add-on via the Hutch app or by dialing 131#

[10. Can this plan be used on different Devices ?](https://hutch.lk/gaurawa-plan)

This plan is exclusively intended for smartphone use.
