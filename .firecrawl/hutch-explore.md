## Explore Hutch

HUTCH, one of Sri Lanka's leading mobile operators, connects over 95% of Sri Lankans with its strong island-wide Hutch 4G network. Offering the best value for money on data and calls, HUTCH empowers users with seamless connectivity nationwide, ensuring fast and efficient communication.

As part of CK Hutchison Holdings, a global conglomerate with operations in over 50 markets, HUTCH benefits from world-class expertise and technological innovation. The Group's telecommunications division serves over 150 million subscriptions worldwide, with 3 Group Europe operating in the UK, Italy, Sweden, Denmark, Austria, and Ireland.

For more information on CK Hutchison Holdings, visit [www.ckh.com.hk/en/global/home.php](https://www.ckh.com.hk/en/global/home.php).
