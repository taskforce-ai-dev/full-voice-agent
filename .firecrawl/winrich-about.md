# About Us

![](https://winrich.lk/storage/uploads/images/original/1766554309_694b7ac5749ae.png)

Company Profile

## Who We Are?

Winrich International is a trusted and leading Sri Lankan organization specializing in the import and
distribution of premium-quality commercial kitchen and bakery equipment. We are the authorized
distributor for several globally recognized manufacturers, providing certified, warranty-backed
solutions to the HoReCa industry nationwide.

Our Origin

## Why We Started

Winrich was born from a deep understanding of a gap: the lack of dependable suppliers and after-sales support in Sri Lanka’s commercial kitchen and bakery equipment space. With a clear mission to elevate the industry standard.
