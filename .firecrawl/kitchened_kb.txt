KITCHENED' is a Sri Lankan distributor of commercial kitchen and bakery equipment, serving the HoReCa industry and food businesses island-wide. KITCHENED' supplies high-quality, globally certified machines and tools to bakeries, hotels, restaurants, cafes, educational institutions, and government facilities across Sri Lanka. The company is an authorized distributor for several internationally recognized manufacturers.

KITCHENED' serves businesses across multiple industries in Sri Lanka. These include hotels and catering operations, bakeries and confectionery businesses, restaurants and cafes, educational institutes such as schools and universities, government and public sector institutions, supermarkets, and corporate food facilities. KITCHENED' provides tailored equipment solutions to each sector based on their specific scale, workflow, and hygiene requirements.

KITCHENED' provides a full end-to-end service from consultation through to long-term maintenance. The four core services are: Consultation and Equipment Selection, Procurement and Delivery, Installation and Commissioning, and After-Sales and Maintenance. Customers do not need to manage multiple vendors — KITCHENED' handles the entire process as a single accountable partner.

KITCHENED' offers a free consultation and equipment selection service. Before any purchase, KITCHENED' specialists work with the client to understand their menu, production volumes, workflow, staffing, and available space. The team then designs an equipment lineup that is practical, scalable, and suited to the kitchen or bakery. This includes site assessments, workflow planning, capacity calculations, equipment sizing, and guidance on certified global brands and configurations.

KITCHENED' manages the full procurement and delivery process. Once equipment is selected, KITCHENED' coordinates directly with global manufacturers, monitors production and shipping timelines, and handles all import and local delivery logistics. Every unit is inspected before delivery to verify models, voltages, and accessories match the approved specification. Equipment arrives complete, correctly specified, and on time.

KITCHENED' provides professional installation and commissioning by trained technicians. The technical team handles on-site positioning and leveling, utility connections for power, gas, water, and exhaust, commissioning and performance testing to manufacturer standards, safety and calibration checks, and basic operator training. A full handover package including documentation, manuals, and warranty information is provided.

KITCHENED' provides island-wide after-sales and maintenance support. The after-sales team covers warranty support, priority breakdown response, scheduled preventive maintenance visits, and genuine spare parts supply. KITCHENED' carries ample stock of genuine spare parts for all the equipment it supplies. Technicians follow manufacturer guidelines to minimize downtime and extend equipment lifespan. Support is available throughout Sri Lanka, including remote locations.

KITCHENED' equipment is certified to global standards. Products supplied by KITCHENED' carry certifications including UL Solutions, ISO 9001, and CE marking. These certifications confirm that the machines meet international quality, safety, and performance standards. KITCHENED' is an authorized distributor, so all equipment comes with valid manufacturer warranties.

KITCHENED' supplies a wide range of commercial ovens. The oven range includes electric convection ovens (four-tray capacity), gas deck ovens in multiple configurations (one deck one tray, one deck two trays, one deck three trays, two deck four trays, two deck six trays, three deck nine trays), electric pizza deck ovens, and rotary ovens with sixteen or thirty-two tray capacity. The rotary oven is suited for high-volume bakery production. Customers can request a quote for any oven model.

KITCHENED' supplies dough and food mixers in multiple capacities. The mixer range includes dough mixers in twenty-kilogram sixty-litre three-phase and fifty-litre configurations, and food mixers in twenty-litre, thirty-litre, and forty-litre bowl sizes including Euro-grade models with extra bowls. These mixers are designed for commercial bakeries, hotels, and food production operations that require consistent and reliable mixing.

KITCHENED' supplies a full range of dough preparation equipment. This includes proofers with sixteen-tray capacity for controlled dough fermentation, dough dividers that divide dough into thirty-six equal pieces, dough sheeters in both table-top and floor-standing configurations, dough moulders, and bread slicers with thirty-one blades for uniform slicing of baked loaves. These machines cover the complete bakery production line from mixing through to final slicing.

The range includes soft-serve ice cream machines with forty to fifty litre per hour output, an H-model soft-serve machine with twenty to twenty-eight litre per hour output, hard ice cream machines, fry ice cream machines, and ice cone baking machines. These are suited for hotels, restaurants, cafes, and dessert businesses.
