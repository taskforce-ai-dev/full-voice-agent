## Frequently Asked Questions

*   [What are the benefits of Hutch Unlimited night time Data Plans?](javascript:void(0))Unlimited Night Time Data Plans will offer you absolute freedom to Download or Stream without worrying about burning out your Standard data. 
*   [Can I use these Plans anytime of the Day?](javascript:void(0))Unlimited Night Time Data plans are valid to be used only between 12am & 8am. 
*   [Are Unlimited Plans available for both 72 & 78?](javascript:void(0))Yes, Unlimited Data Plans are available for both 078 & 072 users. 
*   [Can this plan be used on different Devices ?](javascript:void(0))This plan is exclusively intended for smartphone use. 

*   [Are Unlimited Plans available for both Pre-paid & Post-paid users?](javascript:void(0))No, Unlimited Night Time Data plans are available ONLY for Pre-paid users as Add-on plans. 
*   [How do I activate an Unlimited Data Plan?](javascript:void(0))You can activate any of these Plans simply via the Hutch Self-care App or by dialing *131# USSD Menu. 
*   [Are these Plans truly Unlimited?](javascript:void(0))Yes, You can use these Plans without any Quota or speed limitations.
