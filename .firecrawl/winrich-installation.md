# Installation &  Commissioning

![](https://winrich.lk/storage/uploads/images/original/1768804007_696dcea7d02f4.png)[Request a Consultation](https://winrich.lk/contact-us)

Getting the right equipment is only half the job — installing it correctly is what protects
your investment.

Once your equipment is selected, our technical team takes over to ensure every unit is
installed, tested and ready for real-world use. Winrich technicians coordinate with your
site team to position the equipment, connect utilities and run full test cycles according
to manufacturer standards. From ovens and mixers to steamers and dispensers, we
make sure each machine operates safely, efficiently and exactly as specified before
handover.
Our goal is simple: a smooth, hassle-free installation so your team can start using the
equipment with confidence from day one.

###### What we offer

- On-site delivery, positioning and leveling of equipment
- Utility checks and connections (power, gas, water, exhaust, as applicable)
- Commissioning and performance testing to manufacturer standards
- Safety, calibration and control checks for optimal operation
- Basic operator training on daily use and care
- Handover of documentation, manuals and warranty information

###### Why Choose Our Service?

- Reduced risk of equipment damage or voided warranties
- Maximized equipment efficiency and lifespan
- Ensured staff safety and operational confidence
- Seamless integration into your daily workflow

###### Result

Equipment that is correctly installed, fully tested and ready for service — reducing
downtime, avoiding early breakdowns and protecting your warranty and long-term
performance.
