## Frequently asked questions

[1. What are 'Oneload' plans?](https://hutch.lk/one-load/)

Oneload plans offer you the chance to stay connected for a whole year with a single recharge. You need not worry about monthly recharges, pack expiries, running out of balances etc. now as you will be connected all year round.

[2. What are the benefits of 'Oneload' plans?](https://hutch.lk/one-load/)

Oneload plans are designed as bundle plans that offer you both Voice & Data benefits. Under the umbrella of ‘Oneload’ we offer 4 plans that consists of Anytime Data, Most popular social media plans & Any net calls.

You simply select the plan that best suites your mobile need.

[3. Who can activate 'Oneload' plans?](https://hutch.lk/one-load/)

Any Hutch customer can activate ‘Oneload’ plans.

– Prepaid customers can simply activate the plan as an add on the number.

– Existing Postpaid customers will have to request for a package change.

[4. How can I activate 'Oneload' plans on my existing Hutch connection?](https://hutch.lk/one-load/)

If Prepaid – You can activate the respective plan of your choice by recharging pack price, via Hutch APP or USSD code *131#.

If Postpaid – You may request for a package change via an email, Hutch app (Support request), Hutch.lk Chat service, contact center (1788) or by visiting a Hutch shop

[5. How do I get a new connection with a Oneload plan?](https://hutch.lk/one-load/)

The easiest method is to order your connection via Hutch.lk as physical SIM with doorstep delivery or as an e-SIM.

You also have the option of picking up a DIY connection from Keells / Food city supermarkets or by visiting the nearest retailer shop or Hutch shop.

[6. What is the validity of these plans?](https://hutch.lk/one-load/)

These plans are designed to offer you validity of 360 days (12 x 30 day cycles)

[7. How often will my benefits renew?](https://hutch.lk/one-load/)

The monthly benefit entitlements will be renewed every 30 days from the date of activation.

For example, If the plan is activated on the 1st of January, benefits for the 1st month will be enabled immediately and then on 31st Jan the benefits for 2nd cycle will be added.

[8. What If I don’t use my data & voice balances in a month?](https://hutch.lk/one-load/)

These plans allow to roll over the unused my data & voice balances. They automatically carry over and add to your next month’s benefits.

For example, If the plan is activated on the 1st of January, benefits for the 1st month will be enabled immediately and then on 31st Jan the benefits from 2nd cycle will be added on top of unused balance.

[12.Can I use this Plan on 3G & 4G?](https://hutch.lk/one-load/)

Yes, these plans will be valid for both Hutch 4G & 3G networks without restriction based on availability.

[13.Are these plans available for 078 & 072 customers?](https://hutch.lk/one-load/)

Yes, they are available for 072 & 078 Customers.

[14.Are these plans available for both Prepaid / Postpaid customers?](https://hutch.lk/one-load/)

Yes, they are available both prepaid & postpaid users. However, it is available as an add-on for prepaid customers & only as a base package for postpaid customers)

[15. What if finished my Oneload quota ?](https://hutch.lk/one-load/)

If you run out of any quota benefits offered, you may activate a onetime add on plan from our Anytime plan range or any net voice plans.

[16.Can I activate another plan while having a Oneload plan?](https://hutch.lk/one-load/)

Yes. You can activate multiple plans while having Oneload plan.

[17.How can I check my balance?](https://hutch.lk/one-load/)

You can check your balance form Hutch Self-care App or by Dialing a USSD code. For prepaid dial *344# & post *444#.

[18. Do I need to keep a refundable deposit for postpaid plan?](https://hutch.lk/one-load/)

There is no deposit requirement to purchase a Oneload post paid plan since the rental is to be paid when obtaining the connection.

[19.Can I switch between Oneload plans after using a few months?](https://hutch.lk/one-load/)

Yes, you can switch to a plan that suites your needs better.

If you are upgrading to a higher priced plan, you will be refunded on prorate basis for the remaining months & the balance shortfall for the new plan must be paid for the package to take effect.

In the event that you wish to downgrade, the refund will be made on prorated basis & the rental of the new plan will be applied

[20.When will my Oneload postpaid renew?](https://hutch.lk/one-load/)

Your Oneload plan will renew at the end of the 12th month. Hence you need to ensure that your account has sufficient balance for the plan to renew.

[21. Do I receive a bill on every month?](https://hutch.lk/one-load/)

You will receive a bill at the end of each calendar month with any transactions that you have made in the month.

if you have utilized within your monthly benefits there will be no payable outstanding mentioned in the bill.

[22.What are the eligibility criteria for the money-back guarantee?](https://hutch.lk/one-load/)

– New connection activated with “Oneload” plan

– A refund request should be made within 7 days from the date of SIM activation via 8800.

– Maximum usage should be equal or less than 2GB or 30 Min calls or 30 SMS

[23. How can I request for a refund?](https://hutch.lk/one-load/)

You should a send SMS to 8800 with following details.

Hutch Number

NIC

alternate number

[24.How do I receive a refund money?](https://hutch.lk/one-load/)

You can receive your refund as a reload / payment to your alternate number or a bank transfer.

[25. How long it will take to receive the refund?](https://hutch.lk/one-load/)

Within maximum of 14 days you will receive your refund.
