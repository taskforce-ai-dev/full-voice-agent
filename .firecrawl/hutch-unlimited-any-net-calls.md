## FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

*   [1. Who can activate these plans?](javascript:void(0))The Rs. 599 monthly plan is available for both prepaid and postpaid Hutch customers, while the Rs. 189 weekly plan is available for prepaid customers only.  
*   [2. What benefits do these plans offer?](javascript:void(0))These plans come with unlimited Any-Net voice calls, Anytime Data, and SMS benefits as shown below: 
| Item | Plan 1 | Plan 2 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Plan Price (Rs.) | 599/- | 189/- |
| Validity (Days) | 30 | 7 |
| Prepaid or Post-paid | Both | Only Pre-paid |
| Any Time Data (GB) | 3 | 1 |
| Any Net Voice Minutes | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Number of SMSs | 500 | 125 | 
*   [3. What’s the special benefit of these plans ?](javascript:void(0))You can enjoy unlimited Any-Net calls throughout the plan validity period.  

*   [4. How can these plans be activated?](javascript:void(0))You can activate these plans easily via the Hutch App, USSD (*141#), or by recharging the exact pack value.  
*   [5. Can these plans be used in different devices?](javascript:void(0))These plans are exclusively intended for smartphone use.
