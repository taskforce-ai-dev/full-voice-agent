# Procurement &  Delivery

![](https://winrich.lk/storage/uploads/images/original/1768803922_696dce52908b0.png)[Request a Consultation](https://winrich.lk/contact-us)

Once the plan is agreed, we take care of getting the right machines to your site, on time and in perfect condition.

After you finalise your equipment selection, Winrich manages the entire ordering and
logistics process for you. We coordinate with global manufacturers, monitor production
and shipping timelines, and handle all import and local delivery arrangements. Every
unit is checked against your approved specification – from model and voltage to
accessories – before it leaves our custody.
Our aim is to give you a smooth, predictable supply process, with clear communication
at every stage and no last-minute surprises when your project is ready for installation.

###### Our Procurement Expertise

- Order processing
- Lead-time planning and delivery schedules aligned with your project timeline
- Secure transport, handling and storage of equipment prior to installation
- Pre-delivery inspection to verify models, voltages and accessories
- Regular updates on order status and shipment progress

###### Why Partner With Us?

- Mitigated risk through strict compliance and proven processes
- Streamlined procurement with a single, accountable supplier
- Assurance of quality and reliability with certified global brands
- On-time, on-budget delivery for public projects

###### Result

Equipment that arrives complete, correctly specified and on time – ready for installation
and commissioning, without delays or documentation issues.
