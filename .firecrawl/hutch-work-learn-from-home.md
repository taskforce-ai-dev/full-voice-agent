## FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

*   [Can I use these plan on any Network?](javascript:void(0))Yes, these plans will be valid for both Hutch 4G & 3G networks without restriction. 
*   [Are these plans available for 078 & 072 customers?](javascript:void(0))Yes, they are available for all 078 & 072 subscribers on the Hutch network. 
*   [Are these plans available for both Prepaid / Postpaid customers?](javascript:void(0))Currently, the Study from Home – Buddy plans (Rs 41 / Rs 91) are available only for prepaid users. We will soon enable it for postpaid users to enjoy as well. The Work & Study from Home plan (Rs 717) is available for both Prepaid & Postpaid user as add-on plans. 
*   [How will this plan work?](javascript:void(0))When you activate any of the above mentioned plans, you will be entitled for a specific Anytime quota and a validity. The quota offered will ONLY allow access to the list of applications allowed for each plan. However YouTube will be offered ONLY on the Rs 717 plan at a video resolution of 360p. 
*   [Why can’t I access any other sites or app when I have this plan?](javascript:void(0))The Data bundle offered on Work & Study plans will allow you access to the list apps & URLs offered for the plan. If you wish to access in other app/site, you would need to activate a standard data bundle which will give you un-restricted access to the internet such as Day/Night or Anytime plans.  

*   [What If I activate an Unlimited YouTube plan or Unlimited Facebook + Whatsapp plan while having this plan?](javascript:void(0))When accessing YouTube or WhatsApp, the unlimited plans will take priority over this plan. Therefore the experience will adjust according to the product specifications of the Unlimited YouTube or Unlimited Facebook + WhatsApp plans. 
*   [What if I have a cliQ plan activated on the app while having this plan?](javascript:void(0))cliQ plan will take priority over this plan. Therefore you will be access the benefits of this plan only once the cliQ plan validity has expired. 
*   [Can I use this plan on mobile hotspot?](javascript:void(0))Yes, You can. However if you are using the same application from more than one device in the hotspot simultaneously, you may experience some buffering or loading issues. 
*   [Can I use these plans on a Router?](javascript:void(0))Yes, You can. However if you are using the same application from more than one device in the hotspot simultaneously, you may experience some buffering or loading issues. 
*   [Why am I getting charged from Normal quota plan or main balance when I am using Zoom, Teams or Meets while having a work & study plan?](javascript:void(0))You will need to ensure that the necessary permissions have been granted for the applications to operate in its normal pattern. Therefore please check whether you have granted permission for applications such as ‘Zoom, Teams or Meets’ to use Microphone & Audio functions of the device you use to connect. If this continues after allowing necessary permissions, please contact us on 1788. 
*   [Can this plan be used on different Devices ?](javascript:void(0))This plan is exclusively intended for smartphone use.
