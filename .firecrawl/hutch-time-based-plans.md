## Frequently asked questions

 1. What are Hutch time-based unlimited data plans? 

 Hutch Time-based Unlimited Data Plans are time-based plans that provide unlimited data access for the selected validity period. 

 2. How do time-based unlimited data plans work? 

 Once you activate a time-based unlimited data plan, you get unlimited data access for the plan’s validity period. The plan automatically expires when the validity period ends. 

 3. What time-based unlimited data plans are available and what are the prices? 

 Hutch offers three Hourly Plan options: 
| Plan | Price (Rs.) | Validity (Hours) | Data Limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Plan 1 | Rs. 55 | 1 hour | Unlimited |
| Plan 2 | Rs. 95 | 2 hours | Unlimited |
| Plan 3 | Rs. 185 | 5 hours | Unlimited |
 All prices are inclusive of taxes. 

 4. Who can activate a time-based unlimited data plan? 

 These plans are available only for prepaid customers. 

 5. How do I activate a time-based unlimited data plan? 

 You can activate a time-based unlimited data plan through the Hutch App only. 

 6. Can I buy multiple time-based unlimited data plans at once? 

 Yes. You can activate multiple time-based plans back-to-back. Each plan is valid from the time of activation and will run for its own validity period separately. 

 7. Can I use time-based unlimited data plans for streaming and video? 

 Absolutely! Time-based unlimited data plans provide unlimited data during their validity period, so you can browse, stream, download, and scroll without extra charges—within the plan time frame. 

 8. What happens when the plan expires? 

 Once the validity ends, your internet will revert to standard pay-as-you-go data rates (if available) or your next active data pack. 

 9. Is there any fair usage policy? 

 No. There is no Fair Usage Policy (FUP) applied to the Hourly Plans. You will enjoy unlimited data access throughout the full validity period of the selected plan. 

 10. Can time-based unlimited data plans be used on all devices? 

 This plan is exclusively intended for smartphone use.
