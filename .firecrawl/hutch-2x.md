## Frequently asked questions

[1. What is Hutch 2X plans?](https://hutch.lk/2x/)

These plans are designed to offer you best value in the market for both your Mobile & Homebroadband needs.

[2. What are the benefits of 'Hutch 2X' plans?](https://hutch.lk/2x/)

This plan gives you the ability to save upto 60% by managing Mobile & Home broadband needs in one connection. No Router costs or Set-up fees required as you can simply connect your home devices to your Smartphone with Hotspot Wi-Fi.

[3. Whats is Hotpot WiFi?](https://hutch.lk/2x/)

Hotspot Wi-Fi is a feature which is available in Smartphones. You can convert your smartphone device into a Wi-Fi router for your home or mobile needs.

[4. How can I enable Hotspot on my device](https://hutch.lk/2x/)

**For android users**

 -Swipe down from the top of the screen.

 -Tap Hotspot.

 If you don’t find Hotspot, at the bottom left, tap Edit and drag Hotspot into your Quick Settings.

**For IOS users**

 Go to Settings > Mobile services > Personal Hotspot

[5. How to connect another device to your phone's hotspot?](https://hutch.lk/2x/)

1. On the other device, open that device’s list of Wi-Fi options.

 2. Pick your phone’s hotspot name.

 3. Enter your phone’s hotspot password.

 4. Click Connect.

[6. How many devices can I connect at once with Hotspot WiFi plans.](https://hutch.lk/2x/)

Android device allow you to connect upto 10 devices while iOS allows only 5 devices. However depending on the bandwith usage on each device user experience may vary.

[7. Are these plans available for both Prepaid / Postpaid customers?](https://hutch.lk/2x/)

Yes, they are available both prepaid & postpaid users.

[8. Who can activate 'Hutch 2X' plans?](https://hutch.lk/2x/)

Any new or existing Hutch customer can activate ‘Hutch 2X’ plans.

 – Prepaid customers can simply activate the plan as an add-on to the same number.

 – Postpaid customers can obatin the connection as monthly subscription plan or activate as a recurring or one-time add-on plan.

[9. How can I activate 'Hutch 2X' plans on my existing Hutch connection?](https://hutch.lk/2x/)

If Prepaid – You can activate a plan by recharging pack price, via Hutch APP

If postpaid – You may activate respective add on plan via Hutch App or request for a package change via Hutch app or email

[10. How do I get a new connection with a Hutch 2X plan?](https://hutch.lk/2x/)

The easiest method is to order your connection via Hutch.lk as physical SIM with doorstep delivery or as an e-SIM. You also have the option of picking up a DIY connection from Keells / Food city supermarkets or by visiting the nearest retailer shop or Hutch shop.

[11. Can I activate another plan while having a Hutch 2X plan?](https://hutch.lk/2x/)

Yes. You can activate multiple plans while having Hutch 2X plan.

[12. Can I have Hutch 2X plan while having a Nonstop social media plan?](https://hutch.lk/2x/)

Yes. You can activate a Hutch 2X plan while having a Nonstop social media plan. However, when you are accessing the social media apps offered on the nonstop social media plan it will be at SD quality. Hence we do not recommend you to activate Hutch 2X plans together with Nonstop Social media plans.

[13. What if finished my Hutch 2X quota ?](https://hutch.lk/2x/)

If you run out of any Data quota benefits offered, you may activate a special Hutch 2X Add-on plan for in the interim period.

[14. How can I check my balance?](https://hutch.lk/2x/)

You can check your balance form Hutch Self-care App or by Dialing a USSD code, Dial *344# for Prepaid or Dial *444# for Postpaid.

[15 .Is there an extreme user limit applicable on the Unlimited Rs. 4,999 plan?](https://hutch.lk/2x/)

This plan is designed to offer you a truly non-stop experience for both your Mobile data usage & Hotspot needs. However, upon reaching 250GB the connection will be stepped down to a 5Mbps max speed which will be sufficient for 1080p HD Video streaming.
