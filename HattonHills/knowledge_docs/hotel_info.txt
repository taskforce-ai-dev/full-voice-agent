2. ACCOMMODATION

Occupancy and "Double" Rooms:
A "double room" means double occupancy — accommodation for two guests. Hatton Hills does not have a separate "double room" category; every room type is a private standalone unit, priced by occupancy.

For a double (two guests), the Suites are the right choice: Forest Escape, Eco Harmony, and Sunrise Vista each comfortably accommodate up to two guests on a double-occupancy basis. So when a guest asks for a double room, recommend one of the Suites.

For larger groups, the Chalets — Mount Luxe and Mount Monarch — each accommodate up to five guests.

Basis: All rates are on DBL HB (Half Board — Breakfast and Dinner included)
