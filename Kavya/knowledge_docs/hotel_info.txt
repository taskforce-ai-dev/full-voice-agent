Mosvold Boutique Hotels is a family founded hospitality group on Sri Lanka's Southern Coast, with properties along the stretch from Balapitiya to Ahangama. The founders are Nimal Martinus, Founder and Chairman, Nilanka Martinus, Founder and Managing Director, and Kurt Mosvold, Founder and Deputy Chairman. The first Mosvold hotel opened in Ahangama in 2012. The region is known for devil mask making, wood carving, surfing, tea and cinnamon plantations, and turtle hatcheries.

Mosvold Boutique Hotels operates two properties that guests can book. The first is Mosvold Villa in Ahangama, and the second is Sundara by Mosvold in Balapitiya. The two properties are separate hotels with different addresses, different room types and different experiences, so when a guest enquires it is important to confirm which of the two they mean. The reservations hotline for the group is 0094 77 335 8800.
