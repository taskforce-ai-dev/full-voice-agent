Agent Deep Dive

Workflow Automation & Voice AI

UNIT 01

AI Workflow Agents

These agents sit inside your operational flow — monitoring inboxes, CRMs, and databases — and take autonomous action on routine tasks. They don't just notify; they execute. When a new lead enters your CRM, the agent qualifies it, sends a personalized follow-up, schedules the next touchpoint, and updates your pipeline — all within seconds, not days.
  • Email Autoreply System — Context-aware responses that match tone, answer questions, and escalate when needed
  • Lead Generation & Follow-up — Captures, scores, nurtures, and hands off qualified leads to your sales team
  • Customer Onboarding — End-to-end automation of welcome flows, document collection, and account setup
How It Works

Integrates with your CRM (HubSpot, Salesforce, Zoho), email (Gmail, Outlook), and databases via API. Rules and triggers are configured during onboarding. The agent operates autonomously with escalation rules for edge cases that need human judgment.

UNIT 02

Voice Agents

These are not IVR menus or "press 1 for sales" bots. Our voice agents hold natural, flowing conversations with callers — understanding intent, answering complex questions from a knowledge base, and taking real actions like creating bookings or looking up orders. They speak in the caller's language with human-like intonation and can handle interruptions gracefully.
  • After-Hours Customer Service — Never miss a call. Full conversational support while your team is offline
  • Appointment Booking & Confirmation — Checks live availability, books slots, sends confirmations in real-time
  • Lead Qualification Calls — Asks structured questions, scores leads, routes qualified prospects to closers
How It Works

Powered by LLMs (Claude, GPT-4) with Twilio telephony. The agent receives calls, uses speech-to-text to understand the caller, generates intelligent responses, and converts them back to natural speech. Integrates with your booking system, CRM, or any API. Supports multilingual conversations (Arabic, English, French, Sinhala, Tamil).

Agent Deep Dive

Document Intelligence & Business Analytics

UNIT 03

Document Processing

Every organization is drowning in documents — invoices, purchase orders, shipping manifests, compliance forms, HR paperwork. Our document agent reads any format (PDF, scanned image, email attachment), extracts structured data with near-perfect accuracy, validates it against your rules, and pushes it into your accounting, ERP, or HR system. What took a human 15 minutes takes the agent 8 seconds.
  • Shipping & Logistics — Bills of lading, customs forms, and freight invoices processed and routed automatically
  • HR Onboarding — Employment contracts, ID verification, tax forms extracted and filed into your HRIS
  • Supplier Invoices to Xero / MYOB — Line items, tax codes, and totals extracted and posted directly to your accounting software
How It Works

Uses OCR and LLM-based extraction to understand document structure regardless of layout. Validation rules catch anomalies (duplicate invoices, mismatched totals). Connects to Xero, MYOB, SAP, or any system via API. Exceptions are flagged for human review rather than silently passing through.

UNIT 04

Business Intelligence

Most businesses have data everywhere — CRM, accounting, marketing tools, spreadsheets — but no unified view. Our BI agent connects all your data sources, builds real-time dashboards, and doesn't just show you what is happening — it tells you why. You get cashflow forecasts, pipeline velocity, and marketing attribution without hiring a data analyst.
  • Real-time Sales Pipeline — See deal progression, conversion rates, and forecasted revenue across all channels
  • Cashflow Forecasting — Predict inflows/outflows with early warning alerts for liquidity risk
  • Marketing ROI by Channel — Attribution modeling that shows which spend drives actual revenue, not just clicks
How It Works

Connectors pull data from Salesforce, HubSpot, Xero, Google Analytics, Meta Ads, and more. Data is normalized and modeled in a warehouse. Dashboards update in real-time with drill-down capability. Alerting rules notify stakeholders when KPIs deviate from targets.

Custom Software & Team-Facing Applications

UNIT 05

Custom Software

Off-the-shelf software forces you to adapt your business to someone else's workflow. We build the opposite — applications designed from the ground up for your specific industry, processes, and team structure. Whether it's a quoting tool that understands your pricing logic or a field service app that manages your technicians' schedules, every feature exists because your business needs it.
  • Industry-Specific Quoting — Configure complex pricing rules, material costs, and margins with auto-generated proposals
  • Field Service Management — Dispatch, track, and manage mobile technicians with GPS, job status, and client sign-off
  • Multi-Location Platform — Centralize operations across branches with role-based access and consolidated reporting
How It Works

We scope, design, and build web and mobile applications using modern stacks (React, Next.js, Python). The software integrates with your existing ecosystem via APIs. We handle hosting, maintenance, and iterative improvements based on real usage data post-launch.

UNIT 06

AI Micro Apps

Not every problem needs a full software platform. Micro apps are lightweight, focused interfaces that solve one specific operational pain point for your staff. An employee submits an expense with a photo of the receipt — the AI extracts the data, categorizes it, and routes it for approval. A manager approves leave requests from a single dashboard. These are fast to build, fast to deploy, and immediately impactful.
  • Expense Submission App — Photograph receipt, AI extracts fields, auto-categorizes, routes to approver in seconds
  • Leave Request Dashboard — Staff submit requests, managers approve/decline, balances update in real-time
  • Document Processing Portal — Upload any document, AI classifies and extracts, routes to correct department
How It Works

Each micro app is a standalone web application accessible from any device. AI handles the data extraction and classification layer. Approval workflows and notifications are built in. Typically deployed within 2–4 weeks from kickoff, with ongoing refinement based on your team's feedback.
