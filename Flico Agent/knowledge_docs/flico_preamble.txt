RODRIGO REALTORS — RENTAL PROPERTY KNOWLEDGE BASE
==================================================

Rodrigo Realtors is a trusted Sri Lankan real estate agency specialising in rental properties across the City of Colombo. The current rental portfolio is made up of one-bedroom and two-bedroom apartments and houses — compact, ready-to-occupy homes suited to single professionals, couples, and small families. Whether you are looking for a furnished city apartment or a small house with a garden, a dedicated team of Rodrigo Realtors consultants is ready to guide you through every step of the rental process.

When you express interest in a property, a Rodrigo Realtors salesperson will personally contact you to confirm the full details, answer your questions, and arrange a convenient viewing appointment. Rodrigo Realtors does not send property details, photographs, or documents to callers directly; a salesperson always follows up in person. Callers are welcome to ask about properties by area (Colombo zone), property type, monthly budget, number of bedrooms, or furnishing.

Every property in the current portfolio is available now and has a fixed, quoted monthly rent. Rentals are let on standard lease terms: a refundable deposit of two to three months, one month of rent paid in advance, and a minimum lease period of one year. Every property includes allocated parking; the number of parking spaces is noted per property.

AREAS COVERED
-------------

Rodrigo Realtors' current rental portfolio covers six postal zones in the City of Colombo. Colombo 2 (Slave Island and Union Place) is home to the city's flagship luxury towers and is a short walk from the business district. Colombo 3 (Kollupitiya) is a premium, sea-facing district close to embassies. Colombo 5 (Havelock Town and Narahenpita) is a family-friendly area anchored by Havelock City. Colombo 6 (Wellawatte) sits close to the beach. Colombo 7 (Cinnamon Gardens) is Colombo's most prestigious, leafy, embassy-lined neighbourhood. Colombo 8 (Borella) is a busy urban district near major hospitals.
