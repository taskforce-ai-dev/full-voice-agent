STAR PROPERTIES — RENTAL PROPERTY KNOWLEDGE BASE
=================================================

Star Properties is a trusted Sri Lankan real estate agency specialising in rental properties across the City of Colombo. The portfolio covers apartments, houses, and commercial and office space — ranging from compact one-bedroom homes to large five-bedroom houses, and from small office units to whole commercial buildings. A dedicated team of Star Properties consultants guides callers through every step of the rental process.

When you express interest in a property, a Star Properties salesperson will personally contact you to confirm the full details, answer your questions, and arrange a convenient viewing appointment. Star Properties does not send property details, photographs, or documents to callers directly; a salesperson always follows up in person. Callers are welcome to ask about properties by area (Colombo zone), property type, monthly budget, number of bedrooms, or furnishing.

NOT every property has a published price. Many listings are quoted on request: for those, say plainly that the rent is available on request and that a consultant will confirm it on follow-up. Never estimate one, never infer a figure from a similar property, and never imply a price band. One listing is priced per DAY rather than per month — always state a rent with the exact period recorded for that property, and never present a per-day rate as though it were monthly.

Lease terms vary by property and are NOT uniform across the portfolio. Some listings record a deposit, an advance and a minimum lease; others record none. Quote only the terms recorded for the specific property being discussed, and where none are recorded, say a consultant will confirm them. The same applies to parking: some properties record allocated parking spaces and some do not. Availability also varies — most properties are available now, and one is available soon.

AREAS COVERED
-------------

Star Properties' rental portfolio covers nine postal zones in the City of Colombo. Colombo 1 (Fort and Galle Face) is the corporate hub, with sea views and premium addresses. Colombo 2 (Slave Island, Union Place and Park Street) holds the city's flagship luxury towers, close to the central business district. Colombo 3 (Kollupitiya and Marine Drive) is a premium, sea-facing district close to embassies. Colombo 4 (Bambalapitiya) is central and well served by schools. Colombo 5 (Havelock Town, Narahenpita, Thimbirigasyaya and Kirula Lane) is family-friendly, anchored by Havelock City. Colombo 6 (Wellawatte and Hampden Lane) sits close to the beach. Colombo 7 (Cinnamon Gardens, Barnes Place and Rosmead Place) is Colombo's most prestigious, leafy, embassy-lined neighbourhood. Colombo 8 (Borella, Cotta Road and Castle Street) is a busy urban district near major hospitals. Colombo 10 (Maradana) is commercial.

If a caller names an area outside these nine zones, say plainly that Star Properties has nothing there rather than offering a different area as though it were theirs.

NEXT STEPS
----------

If you are interested in any property listed by Star Properties, simply let Amaya know and she will collect your name and then the best contact number, one at a time, reading each back to confirm. A Star Properties salesperson will then contact you directly to confirm the exact rent and lease terms, share photographs, answer any further questions, and arrange a viewing at a time that suits you. Asking about price, features or specifications is not yet a commitment — details are collected only once a caller shows genuine interest.
