RODRIGO REALTORS — RENTAL PROPERTY KNOWLEDGE BASE
==================================================

Rodrigo Realtors is a trusted Sri Lankan real estate agency specialising in rental properties across the City of Colombo. The rental portfolio covers luxury apartments, family houses, and commercial and office space for rent — from furnished high-rise apartments in landmark towers to ready-to-occupy offices and retail premises. Whether you are looking for a furnished city apartment, a spacious family home, or commercial space for your business, a dedicated team of Rodrigo Realtors consultants is ready to guide you through every step of the rental process.

When you express interest in a property, a Rodrigo Realtors salesperson will personally contact you to confirm the full details, answer your questions, and arrange a convenient viewing appointment. Rodrigo Realtors does not send property details or documents to callers directly; a salesperson always follows up in person. Callers are welcome to ask about properties by area (Colombo zone), property type, monthly budget, number of bedrooms, or furnishing.

Most rentals are let on standard lease terms: a refundable deposit, a few months' rent paid in advance, and a minimum lease period (commonly one year). Several premium listings are quoted as "rent on request" rather than a fixed figure — for these, a Rodrigo Realtors consultant will share the exact monthly rent when they follow up. Many properties include allocated parking; the number of parking spaces is noted per property where known.

AREAS COVERED
-------------

Rodrigo Realtors' rental portfolio is concentrated in the City of Colombo, across postal zones 1 to 10. Colombo 1 (Fort and Galle Face) is the corporate hub, known for sea views and premium high-rise living. Colombo 2 (Slave Island and Union Place) is home to the city's flagship luxury towers, including Altair, One Galle Face, Shangri La, Cinnamon Life, Capitol Twin Peaks and Trizen. Colombo 3 (Kollupitiya) is a premium, sea-facing district close to embassies. Colombo 4 (Bambalapitiya) is central and convenient for schools. Colombo 5 (Havelock Town and Narahenpita) is a family-friendly area anchored by Havelock City. Colombo 6 (Wellawatte) sits close to the beach. Colombo 7 (Cinnamon Gardens) is Colombo's most prestigious, leafy, embassy-lined neighbourhood. Colombo 8 (Borella) is a busy urban district near major hospitals. Colombo 10 (Maradana) is a commercial quarter.
